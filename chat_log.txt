**Getting started in GMod (Garry’s Mod)**

1. **Pick a game mode**  
   * **Sandbox** – the pure “build‑anything‑you‑can‑think” mode.  
   * **Other modes** – TTT, DarkRP, Prophunt, etc.  Choose one that matches your vibe.

2. **Basic controls**  
   * **Move** – WASD, jump (space), crouch (ctrl).  
   * **Physics Gun** – `Q` → pick up or drop objects.  
   * **Tool Gun** – `G` → access a toolbox of building tools (e.g., weld, rope, duplicate).  
   * **Spawn menu** – `Q` → open the “Spawn” panel, click *Spawn* → pick a map or prop.

3. **Spawning stuff**  
   * Press `Q`, go to **“Entities”** → pick a prop, click *Spawn* in the world.  
   * Use the Physics Gun to move, rotate, or duplicate it (`R`).

4. **Building a simple contraption**  
   * Grab a **Weld Tool** from the Tool Gun.  
   * Point at two props → press the left mouse button to weld them together.  
   * Add a **Rope Tool** to make a pully or chain.

5. **Customizing your avatar**  
   * In Sandbox, you’re usually a *player model* you can change by opening the **Spawn** panel → *Player* → choose a model.  
   * For more advanced custom players, you’ll need to create a playermodel in a 3D tool like Blender and export it to GMod – that’s a whole different tutorial.

6. **Having fun**  
   * Experiment: throw a prop into the air, watch it fall, then change its physics.  
   * Invite friends: open the **Friends** menu → “Invite to Game”.  
   * Play with scripts: use **Wiremod** or **Lua** for advanced stuff (but that’s for later).

**Quick tip:** If you get stuck, hit `F1` for the in‑game help, or ask in the community chat. GMod is all about trial and error, so just keep building and have a laugh! 🎮🤖
